## 🧠 Building Intelligent Systems That Can Defend Themselves

I work at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity**, with a focus on transforming advanced research into secure, trustworthy, and deployable intelligent systems.

My research spans **AI Security, Generative AI, Agentic AI, Large Language Models, RAG Security, Adversarial Machine Learning, Explainable AI, Threat Intelligence, Industrial IoT, Edge AI, 5G/6G, Cyber-Physical Systems, Cloud/Fog Security, Software-Defined Networking, and Post-Quantum Cryptography**.

Building intelligent agents that can reason, use tools, retrieve information and operate under security and governance constraints.

Combining machine learning, deep learning, threat intelligence and automated security analytics.

Developing AI systems that are explainable, robust, privacy-aware, secure and suitable for high-impact environments.
