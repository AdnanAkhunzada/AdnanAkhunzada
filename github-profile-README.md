## About

Professor of Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence at the University of Doha for Science and Technology, with 17+ years across the UK, Europe, the Middle East, Asia and New Zealand.

- **Research.** AI security, trustworthy and generative AI, agentic AI evaluation, cyber threat intelligence, adversarial machine learning, and post-quantum cryptography — 200+ peer-reviewed works, 6,250+ citations, h-index 43, i10-index 122.
- **Funding and delivery.** Work Package Lead on EU Horizon 2020 **ASTRID** (EUR 2.93M), Technical Work Package Lead on Vinnova **IIoTSP** (SEK 24M), and Co-PI on **SALLP/SALLPI**, with ABB, Ericsson, Volvo, IBM and Cisco.
- **Invention.** Co-inventor of granted **U.S. Patent 10,581,604 B2**, a post-quantum cryptographic communication protocol.
- **Academic leadership.** Founding Program Director of a B.Sc. in Ethical Hacking and Cybersecurity; authored the MS/PhD research policy behind the M.Sc. in AI and Cognitive Cybersecurity; led ABET accreditation evidence for Data and Cybersecurity.
- **Mentorship.** 9 completed PhDs and 24 research Master's graduates supervised or co-supervised.

> Intelligence without security is fragile. Security without intelligence is limited.
> The future belongs to systems designed with both.
